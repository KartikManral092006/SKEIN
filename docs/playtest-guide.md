# Skein playtest guide

Goal: find out whether new players discover wake riding without being told, and whether the game works well on phones.

## Setup
- Testers: 3–5 people who have never seen the game, each logged into their own Reddit account in the Reddit phone app.
- Send them the link to today's post in r/skein_flow_dev. Check one tester can open it before inviting the rest.
- Ask permission before recording a screen or voice. Keep notes under tester numbers (T1, T2...), not names.

## Success targets (fixed before testing)
- At least 3 of 5 discover wake riding without help.
- At least 3 of 5 land before dark within 3 tries.
- At least 2 of 5 play again without being asked.

## Opening line (say only this)
"This is a game I'm making. I'm testing the game, not you. Please say out loud what you're thinking as you play.
I won't help, because I need to see where it's confusing."

## During play
- Don't explain, hint or point. If asked "what do I do?", reply "what do you think you should do?"
- Step in only if they're stuck for more than 2 minutes, and write down that you did.
- Up to 3 flights each.

## Questions afterwards
1. In your own words, what's the goal?
2. What were the other geese for?
3. What was most confusing or annoying?
4. Would you play tomorrow's leg? Why or why not?

## After all sessions
1. Count how many testers met each target.
2. List problems by how many testers hit each one. Fix the most common first.
3. If most testers never found wake riding, fix that before anything else.

## Ask testers to keep flying
Ask each tester to fly once a day from Dec 1 to Dec 8, so judges see a sky full of real named geese.
